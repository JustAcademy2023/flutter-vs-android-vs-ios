Four files, four philosophies

Same goal, a working mobile app, four genuinely different ways of getting there. Here is what each one asks of you.

flutter.dart

Flutter

One codebase compiled natively for Android, iOS and beyond. Dart is friendlier than most beginners expect, and the instant hot reload keeps early motivation intact.

Which one actually fits you

Less about which framework is "best," more about which one matches how you already think.

  • Want speed and both platforms at onceFlutter gets a working prototype in front of you the fastest.
  • Already comfortable with JavaScriptReact Native turns that existing knowledge into a mobile skill almost immediately.
  • Care about hardware level controlNative Android rewards patience with genuine depth and reliability.
  • Obsessed with polish and detailNative iOS teaches discipline that shows up in every screen you ship.
